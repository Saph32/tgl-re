The ending

From the final boss’s last hit to the screen the game stops on. Every step is read from the code and linked to the next by an address; the whole chain is unchecked on hardware.

Corrects earlier notes. This project said the ending follows corridor 21. It follows corridor 22: after corridor 21 a cutscene plays, $51 becomes 22 (bank 7 $ECDC), and the final boss, It (type $4F, $9151[22]), is fought in a short corridor of its own.
