Why this bank sits where it does

Opens with a single JMP at $8000-$8002 and then falls into what decodes as code at $8003. The one-entry table is the odd one out among the eight banks and has not been explained.

An address on this page does not identify a byte. $8000-$BFFF is the switchable window, so every address below exists in seven banks at once and only bank 4 has these particular bytes at it. Any address taken from a debugger, a wiki or another disassembly has to carry a bank with it before it means anything — tools/whereis.py --bytes settles which bank really holds a given sequence.
