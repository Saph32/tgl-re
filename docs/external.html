What is known about the game from outside the ROM — its manual, for a start — kept one claim at a time, each next to what the ROM says about it.

An outside claim is a lead until the ROM backs it. A manual describes the game its writers meant to ship, in words chosen for players; the ROM is the game. So every entry here carries a status, and unchecked means exactly that. Claims are paraphrased, not copied. The source file is annotations/external.json, which the build validates.

Story

StatusThe source saysThe ROM says
confirmed

An alien world called Naju is heading for Earth. Its surface is the Labyrinth - passages and rooms; inside it is the Dungeon - corridors made by the large alien life forms.

manual-us, p. 3-4

The dialogue script (bank 5 $9639) names NAJU and talks about corridors throughout. The two halves match the ROM's two modes: the 24 x 24 labyrinth map in bank 5 and the 21 scrolling areas in bank 4.

confirmed

The game has two main modes: a vertically scrolling shooter, the corridors, and a top-down adventure, the labyrinth, which is divided into areas.

owner, p. -

The HUD prints AREA from $50 in the labyrinth and from $51 otherwise (bank 7 $E169, bit 1 of $30); a labyrinth savestate shows 'AREA 0 X 8 Y12' with $50 = 0. $51 is the corridor number 1-21 - what this project had been calling 'areas 1-21', CORRECTED. Leaving corridor n sets the labyrinth area to n, folding 11-20 onto 1-10 and capping 21 at 10 (bank 7 $F2A0-$F2AC): corridors n and n+10 belong to labyrinth area n, which is why the keys repeat with period ten.

Goal

StatusThe source saysThe ROM says
consistent

Ten safety devices inside the dungeon must be activated to destroy Naju. Killing the master monster at the end of a corridor may activate part of the safety system, or give a key.

manual-us, p. 6, 7

Area 21's entrance marker is drawn only when areas 1-10 are all flagged in $04A9 (status screen, bank 5 $887F-$888F): ten corridors gate the last one. The dialogue calls them SEALS ('the seal for Corridor No.2', 'Seal No.4 cannot be removed'). Keys are the bits of $04B0, set by bank 7 $F547 with a per-area value from $F6DA. This makes 'area cleared' the natural reading of $04A9 - still a reading, not proven.

Password

StatusThe source saysThe ROM says
consistent

A friendly blue creature in certain labyrinth rooms gives a password if the optional weapon is set to 'NONE' and A is pressed. Enter it via PASSWORD CONTINUE on the title screen.

manual-us, p. 7

The dialogue has the creature's lines ('Do you want me to record your progress? Set your...'). The game's own label for 'none' is NO USE - slot 0 of the weapon cursor, drawn from bank 5 $85FA / $8602 - not NONE.

confirmed

The owner's passwords are written in groups of four using capitals, digits and many lowercase letters with two dots above them (ä, ö, ü, ë ...).

manual-us, p. 15, 18 (handwritten)

CORRECTION PROMPTED BY THIS SOURCE. The project had the grid as 36 characters, A-Z and 0-9. Re-reading the password savestate's nametable shows seven rows of ten: A-Z, 0-3, a-z, 4-9, ! and ? - 64 characters, exactly the six bits a field holds. The lowercase letters are tiles $81-$9A and each of their rows has a row of $AE (two dots) above it, which is why they were written with umlauts. $880F builds the index as column + 10 x row, so 4-9 are 56-61, not 30-35.

inconclusive

The handwritten passwords are real passwords from a real game.

manual-us, p. 15, 18 (handwritten)

Decoding them tests the checksum at bank 3 $859B, but the handwriting is ambiguous (0/O, s/S, 2/z, m/M ...). Over all 1,536 plausible readings of the page-18 password, 6 pass - the 1-in-256 chance rate - and none of 64 readings of page 15's pass. So this does not check the decoder either way. A password typed into an emulator and captured in a savestate would.

consistent

The special password TGL starts the game in a special mode.

owner, p. -

Bank 3 $857A accepts T G L and blanks, skipping the checksum and setting $2F = 0, $30 = 2. The game itself shows the word: press Start on the last ending-credits screen and bank 0 $899F takes one more pass that renders the script PASSWORD TGL ($8BA3). $30 = 2 is the corridors-only game: see the tgl-mode fact.

confirmed

The TGL password plays only the corridors, never the labyrinth, and after each corridor gives items according to the score.

owner, p. -

$30 bit 1 (the back door sets $30 = 2). A new game ($EB18) clears $51 and $77 and starts corridor 0, the opening flight. At a corridor's end $ECE7 tests $30 bit 1: clear, the game returns to the labyrinth ($EB08); set, it far-calls bank 3 $80A0, the YOU GOT ITEM screen, then $ECFC sets $51 = $ED08[$77], increments $77 and starts that corridor. $ED08 is 1, 11, 12, 2, 13, 3, 14, 4, 15, 5, 16, 6, 17, 7, 18, 8, 19, 9, 20, 10, 21 - every corridor once. After 21 the ordinary ending runs. The reward: each corridor start copies the score to $04B9 ($EF86); when the boss comes bank 3 $91A7 turns it into the points gained so far, and during the fight $9305 takes 10 points off every 64 frames. The reward screen adds that to a running total at $04B3 (bank 6 $805A) and prints it; $15 = the total in units of 10,000 points (capped at 255). $80E7 then gives, in order from the saved position $76, every entry of the 73-entry list at $817A whose threshold is at most $15, each through bank 2 $86BC - the ordinary pick-up routine - and prints its threshold followed by 0000. At most five per screen; the rest wait. docs/password.html tabulates the list. Read from the code, NOT played.

Hidden features

StatusThe source saysThe ROM says
confirmed

Holding A and B while pressing Reset opens a sound test.

owner, p. -

Reset runs bank 0 $8332 (JMP entry $800C, called at $FF50). It clears $30 and $2F, reads the pads ($C01E -> $D64C) and at $8341 does LDA $F7 / CMP #$C0 / BNE: held buttons exactly A+B, nothing else. Then it far-calls bank 3 $801B -> $822A, which resets the stack and never returns: it prints SOUND TEST at PPU $2088 and the sound number $15 (starting at 1) in decimal at $20CD. Each frame it takes newly pressed buttons ($C0E4): Right +1 and Left -1, wrapping between 1 and 71 ($47), Down plays sound $15 ($C030 -> bank 0 $97CC), Up far-calls bank 0 $8000 ($97A0, the sound init - it silences everything). 71 is the last song-table entry: the table at $9B04 has 72, and 0 is skipped. This is the only way in - the far-call bytes 03 1A 80 occur once. The same check runs at power-on, since that takes the same path. NOT tried on hardware; read from the code. The key-repeat counter in $14 is dead code: $C0E4 only returns once a button is newly pressed, so every press steps once.

confirmed

Leaving the title screen alone starts a demo, which plays with some levels and items already set up. (The owner asked whether it replays recorded keypresses.)

owner, p. -

How it starts: the title loop (bank 0 $835A) returns when $0300 becomes non-zero, and the title song (song 1) sets it itself - command $94 'set global flag $10' at $9D06 ends the first channel's melody before it loops. The reset path then runs the game setup, bank 0 $805B ($8003); without Start held (and outside the TGL game) it builds the demo state at $8107-$8145: max life $80, every weapon owned at level 2 ($AA in $04AC-$04AE), attack and shield 2, chip level $46 = 6 with full chips, fire rate $6B = 2, a random selected weapon ($3C, 0-11), and $30 bit 7 - the demo flag. $EB18 then starts a random corridor, 0-15 ($F9D1 AND $0F). The input is NOT recorded keypresses, and not an AI either: $DAE1 overwrites the controller bytes every frame with patterns from the table at $DB33, each held for a random 24-40 frames, starting at a random entry - so the ship fires constantly and wanders, and no two demos are alike. It ends after about $1C x 256 frames ($F0/$F1, about two minutes), when the stage ends ($EBA2), or when a real Start is pressed ($F4A3) - each by JMP $FF4A (the timer at $DAED), back to the title. Read from the code; not checked against a savestate.
