Background only — no enemies, ground objects or bosses. Simulated the way $E6CE and $E8AB build it: at each row the records whose trigger has come fire, then the strip's next row goes down and each active overlay slot writes its structure's next row over it, with its delay, repeats, gap, sideways step and edge cut-off.
